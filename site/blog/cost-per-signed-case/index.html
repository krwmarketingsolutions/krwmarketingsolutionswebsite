Turn cost per lead into cost per signed case

Your invoice is priced per lead or per transfer. Your firm is priced per signed case. Those are different units. Comparing two vendors on cost per lead alone will pick the wrong one often enough to matter.

The bridge

Spend is units times price. Signed cases, for leads, are units times the share you actually reach times the share of those conversations that sign. Cost per signed case is spend divided by signed cases. For transfers, skip the contact rate. The transfer is already the conversation. Signed cases are transfers times the share that sign.

Example, not a KRW price

The figures below are round teaching numbers. They are not a quote and they are not a result from a KRW campaign.

100 leads at 75 dollars is 7,500 dollars. You reach 60. Of those, 15 sign. Cost per signed case is 7,500 divided by 15, which is 500 dollars. A transfer offer at 150 dollars that signs one time in four costs 600 dollars per signed case. The lead looks cheaper on the invoice. The transfer is the one to beat only if your sign rate on a live conversation is strong enough. Run your own rates, not ours.

Do the same math before you compare a shared lead at 40 dollars with an exclusive lead at 80 dollars. If the shared lead signs half as often, it was not cheaper.

The Cost Per Case Guide has a longer example and a calculator you can type into. It does not save what you enter. When you know the cost per signed case you want, put it on the intake form.

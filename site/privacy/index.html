Privacy Policy

This policy describes how KRW Marketing Solutions LLC ("KRW", "we", "us") handles personal information. It covers two groups. Law firms, and the people who work at them, when they contact us about buying leads. And consumers who submit information on landing pages we operate.

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Who we are

KRW Marketing Solutions LLC is a performance lead generation company in Los Angeles, California. We generate inquiries for plaintiff side law firms in motor vehicle accident, Social Security disability, and mass tort matters.

Email: kyler@krwmarketingsolutions.com
Phone: (949) 395-2717
Mail: 7046 Hollywood Blvd, Los Angeles, CA 90028

Law firm information

When you send the intake form, email us, or book a call, we collect what you give us. That includes your name, firm name, email, phone, the verticals you want, the states you want, your monthly signed case target, your target cost per case, and your message.

We use this information to reply, to prepare a proposal, to perform a contract if you hire us, and to keep a record of that relationship. We do not sell law firm contact details. We do not share them with other law firms as leads.

Consumer information on our landing pages

We operate landing pages for motor vehicle accident, disability, and mass tort campaigns. If you are a consumer and you submit a form on one of those pages, we collect what you submit. That commonly includes your name, phone number, email address, the state you select, and details about the accident, the condition, or the product you are asking about. Those details can include health or injury information.

We also collect technical data tied to the submission. That includes the page address, the time, your IP address, your browser type, and the consent language that was displayed.

TCPA express written consent

Where a form is used to generate a lead that a law firm may call or text, the form asks for express written consent under the Telephone Consumer Protection Act. The consent box is not prechecked. The language next to the box identifies KRW Marketing Solutions LLC and states that the person agrees to be contacted by KRW and by partner law firms about the legal matter they described. That contact may include automatic dialing systems, prerecorded messages, and text messages. Consent is not a condition of buying a product or of receiving a settlement.

The exact language can vary by campaign. The language that was on screen at the time of the submission is stored with the lead. A consumer can leave the box unchecked. If the campaign requires consent before we pass the lead to a firm, we do not pass that submission on as a callable lead.

Consent certificates

We use ActiveProspect TrustedForm, Jornaya (Verisk), or a similar certificate vendor on consumer forms. The certificate records the page, the time, and the consent language. We store the certificate token with the lead and we pass it to the partner law firm that receives the lead. The certificate vendor processes that session under its own terms.

How we share consumer leads

We share a consumer lead with the partner law firm contracted to receive it. On an exclusive order, that firm is the only firm receiving the lead for the agreed window. We also share data with vendors who host the page, route the lead, place the call, or issue the consent certificate. Those vendors may use the data to provide the service we hired them for.

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act, providing a consumer lead to a law firm for that firm's own intake or marketing may be a sale or a share of personal information. We treat that delivery as a sale or a share. We do not sell the personal information of consumers we know are under 16.

California privacy rights

If you are a California resident, you may ask us to do the following.

  • Tell you the categories and the specific pieces of personal information we collected about you, the sources, the purpose, and the categories of recipients.
  • Correct personal information that is inaccurate.
  • Delete personal information, subject to exceptions the law allows, including records we must keep for a legal claim or a consent dispute.
  • Opt you out of the sale or sharing of personal information.
  • Limit the use of sensitive personal information to what is needed to provide the service you requested.

We will not discriminate against you for exercising these rights. We will not deny a lead submission, charge a different price, or provide a different level of service because you made a request. Campaign forms can still require the fields a law firm needs in order to evaluate a case. That is separate from a privacy request.

Sensitive personal information on consumer forms, including injury or health details you choose to submit, is used to understand the inquiry and to route it to a law firm. We do not use it to build a profile for unrelated advertising.

To make a request, email kyler@krwmarketingsolutions.com with the subject "Privacy request". To opt out of sale or sharing, use the subject "Do Not Sell or Share". Include your name, phone number, and the email you used on the form so we can find the record. We will match those details to verify the request. An authorized agent may submit a request with proof that you gave them permission.

We respond within 45 days. We may take one additional 45 day period when a request is complex, and we will tell you if we do.

Cookies, analytics, and advertising

The company website stores your cookie choice in this browser. We do not set analytics or advertising cookies until you choose Allow on the banner. We also do not load those tools while their IDs in our configuration are still placeholders.

If you allow cookies, and once the IDs are active, the site may use Google Analytics 4, Google Ads, and the Meta Pixel. A scheduling embed loads only after we replace the placeholder scheduler URL, and that vendor may set its own cookies. Details are in the Cookie Policy.

Security

We use access controls on lead systems and encryption in transit when a lead is delivered to a firm. No method of storage or transmission is perfect. If you believe a record was exposed, email us.

Children

Our sites and campaigns are not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 16. If you believe we have, email us and we will delete it.

Other sites

Law firm sites, ad platforms, and certificate vendors have their own policies. This policy covers KRW properties.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top. If we materially change how consumer leads are shared, we will update the consent language on the forms that are affected.

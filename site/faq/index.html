FAQ

Questions firms ask before they buy

Straight answers for managing partners and intake directors. Your order controls if it says something more specific than this page.

What does exclusive mean?

Exclusive means one firm receives that consumer for the matter and the window written in the order. Ask three things before you sign. Does exclusive cover the person, the phone number, or the matter. How many days does it last. What happens if the same person later matches a second campaign you also buy. If those answers are not in the order, you are not buying a defined exclusive.

Do you sell the same lead to more than one firm?

Not on an exclusive order. Shared delivery is a different product. It has to be named in the order, and it should be priced as shared. We do not quietly add a second buyer.

How do returns work?

The window, the reasons that qualify, and the way a credit is applied are in your order. Common reasons are a duplicate, a disconnected number, a lead outside the criteria, or a missing consent record. We do not publish one universal return rate on this site, because the window depends on the product. Ask for the return terms before you start, and review leads inside that window.

How are leads delivered?

A data lead posts to your CRM, your email, or your dialer. A live transfer connects the person to your intake line. Both include the consent record when the campaign collects express written consent. You can read the Cost Per Case Guide to see how those two products turn into a cost per signed case.

How fast does a lead arrive?

Data leads post as the form is submitted. Live transfers connect while the person is still on the phone. The delivery standard for your campaign is written in the order. Figures on the homepage are marked for the owner to confirm and are not a promise until your order says so.

What consent comes with a lead?

When the lead will be called or texted, the consumer page asks for express written consent under the TCPA. The box is not prechecked. We store the language that was on screen, the time, and a TrustedForm or Jornaya certificate, and we send that certificate with the lead. Your firm decides whether to call, text, or accept the person as a client. The Privacy Policy describes this in more detail. It is a draft for counsel.

How is pricing structured?

We price per qualified lead, per live transfer, or per signed case. There is no public rate card and no monthly retainer for media. A quote depends on the vertical, the states, and the criteria your intake team will actually sign. Start on the volume you can take, then scale.

Can we cap volume?

Yes. Daily, weekly, and monthly caps, and the hours you want transfers, are set in the order so intake is not flooded. You can lower a cap when the floor is full. We would rather pause than send leads you cannot call.

Can we choose states?

You name the states. We suppress the rest. You still need to confirm that your firm can take cases there. County or DMA limits can be written into the criteria when the vertical supports them.

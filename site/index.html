Why Choose KRW

The Difference That Drives Results

We're not a lead marketplace or a broker. Every lead we deliver comes from campaigns we built and run ourselves, so quality is our responsibility from first click to signed retainer.

Vertical-Specific Expertise

MVA, SSDI, and mass tort each have different criteria, compliance rules, and economics. We run them as separate operations.

  • Criteria built with your intake team
  • State and docket-level targeting
  • Dedicated campaigns for each vertical
  • Sign-rate reporting by source

Real-Time Routing

Our own routing platform posts leads into your CRM or dialer in seconds, with ping-post, caps, and filters you control.

  • Direct CRM and dialer integrations
  • Daily caps and hour-of-day schedules
  • Duplicate and DNC suppression
  • Live delivery and return dashboards

One Point of Contact

You deal with a person, not a portal. Your account lead manages campaigns, quality, and returns so your team can focus on signing.

  • Dedicated account lead
  • Same-day response on returns
  • Weekly quality reviews
  • Direct line to leadership

Performance Pricing

Pay per qualified lead, transfer, or signed case. No retainers, no minimum ad spend, and a return policy we actually honor.

  • Cost per lead, transfer, or retainer
  • Transparent return windows
  • Start small, scale on results

Compliance First

Because we own the traffic, we control the consent. Every lead carries documented, certified consent. Compliance isn't a checkbox for us — it's the product.

  • TCPA consent on every record

In-House Media Buying

We don't resell anyone else's leads. Our team runs the ads, builds the funnels, and owns every step between the click and your intake line.
